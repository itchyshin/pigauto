| Scenario | Shipped gate (`FALSE`) | Gate sensitivity (`TRUE`) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Nonlinear primary | 16.7% | 13.0% |

These are descriptive means over 10 paired datasets per scenario. They are promising pilot observations, not evidence that the neural branch improves predictions reliably. At this sample size, confirmatory classifications are explicitly `indeterminate: pilot sample below 500`; no efficacy claim is made. The linear-recovery, low-phylogenetic-signal, and null scenarios are retained in the full summary tables under `script/gnn_incremental/results/pilot-20261019-v3-n10-summary/summaries/`. The null scenario showed zero relative reduction in both gate arms. Fully withheld and partly observed species are reported separately in `paired_summary.csv`.

This pilot also exposed a classification defect: the summary code could mark a confirmatory result supported with fewer than the prespecified 500 datasets. A regression assertion first failed on that behavior. The summarizer now holds all confirmatory pilot classifications indeterminate below 500 replicates; `test_accounting.R` and regenerated pilot summaries pass with the correction.

## Limitations

Ten replicates per scenario are insufficient for the planned efficacy assessment or its multiplicity-adjusted decision rule. Results are conditional on the frozen repeated-observation DGP, outer missingness scheme, comparator definitions, and source bundle. They do not establish universal GNN superiority, validate historical releases, or imply that the `TRUE` gate is a supported default. Conformal coverage and other uncertainty guarantees are outside this predictive-value pilot.
