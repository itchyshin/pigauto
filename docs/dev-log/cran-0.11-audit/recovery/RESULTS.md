These are research checks of the workflow, not checks of a frozen final tarball. The drivers are in `script/cran-0.11-recovery/`. The raw RDS files remain under `/home/snakagaw/pigauto_cran_011_audit/` on Totoro. The earlier copies agree with `totoro-sha256.txt` (33 checked, zero mismatches); the final drmTMB copies agree with `final-drm/drm-final-sha256.txt` (24 checked, zero mismatches), which also records ten retained raw-RDS digests.

The prespecified drmTMB Gaussian recovery used n = 300, M = 20 posterior imputations, ten seeds 2026100601 to 2026100610, one response with 60 masked cells per seed, zero true phylogenetic covariance, and the standard four-chain controls. Each seed produced 20 valid downstream fits, independent OLS and SE agreement, independent Rubin arithmetic agreement at the locked tolerances, and successful pooling after fit serialization. The three target coefficients had mean biases +0.0012, -0.0244 and -0.0025. Their 95% Monte Carlo bias intervals were wholly inside the prespecified ±0.15 margin. Coverage counts were 9/10, 10/10 and 10/10; ten seeds do not establish nominal coverage. See `drm-summary.csv` and each seed log and CSV.

This replay validates the final adapter source in this registered regime; it is not an exact-tarball or coverage certificate.

The paired 95% intervals exclude zero in these four generated conditions, and mean Brier differences also favor exact. These results support the current exact route only in this specified simulation regime. See `predictive-pairs-summary.csv` and the per-pair CSV.
