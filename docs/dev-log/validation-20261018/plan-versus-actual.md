## Explicit deviations and evidence handling

The pilot was frozen before later review corrections. Its raw records keep the original source identity. Corrected summaries retain their own evaluator hashes and never rewrite raw identities. These corrections fix summary applicability, pair reporting and input/provenance controls. MAR constructor changes affect a route not selected in that pilot; a separate actual MAR fit passed. Older posterior cell-interval metrics lack requested-cell denominators and remain unavailable for supported interval claims. New production records must carry the strict denominator schema.

The prediction comparator roster is deliberately narrow: current-default GNN-off and mean/mode controls, with the optional Rphylopars arm disabled. Dedicated covariate scenarios exercise GNN contrasts. A broader historical competitor study is outside this implementation.

The corrected follow-up accounts for seventeen successful full-setting attempts, including all twelve analysis cells, tree, AVONET, BIEN, posterior replay and MAR. Original failed attempts remain retained. The revised estimate is 5.5 to 14.8 hours at 48 workers; proposed global stop is fifteen hours. Approval remains pending.

A manifest row or successful one-replicate pilot is not scientific validation. All full-run outcomes, including nonconvergence and unavailable data, must remain in the eventual 32,240-attempt ledger. Entirely absent method/metric keys must be checked against the expected roster even where no result row exists.
