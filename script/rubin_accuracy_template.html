How accurate is each imputation method, beside its coverage?

The campaign page reports coverage. Coverage says whether intervals are honest; it does not say whether the imputed values are close to the truth. This page shows accuracy from the same runs: error of each imputed value, interval score, and error of the downstream slope and correlation.

z-RMSE is the root-mean-square error of the pooled imputation (mean of the 20 draws) on masked cells, divided by the trait's SD; lower is better. Bold marks the lowest in each row. Each row pools ρ = 0 and 0.5.

What this does not cover

Discrete traits (binary, ordinal, categorical) are imputed in the campaign but not scored, so there is no discrete accuracy here. BACE at n = 1000 uses 100 datasets per cell (200 elsewhere), and datasets where a BACE fit errored are left out (see the campaign page's failures table). Numbers come from the campaign aggregate (report.json), the same data as the campaign page.
